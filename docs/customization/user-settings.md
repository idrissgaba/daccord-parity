# User Settings

Open the settings by clicking the **gear icon** in the user bar at the bottom of the sidebar, or from the user bar menu. On a computer they open as a window over the app — close it with the **✕** at the top right, by pressing **Escape**, or by clicking outside it. On a phone each page opens on its own screen.

The sidebar starts with you: click your name (**Edit Profiles**) to edit your profile. Use **Search** to narrow the list to the pages and sections whose name matches. When a page is selected, its sections are listed under it; click one to jump to it.

## Settings Pages

### Account

**Account Info** shows your username and server, with **Edit** to change your profile, and links to switch accounts, manage device profiles, edit a per-server profile (when you are connected to several servers) and, for server administrators, the server administration. **Password & Security** changes your password and sets up two-factor authentication; backup codes are provided for recovery, and you can delete your account from there.

### Data & Privacy

Export your data, leave and delete data, and see what the server retains.

### Notifications

**Overview** turns desktop notifications on or off and suppresses `@everyone` / `@here`. Per-server and per-channel settings live in the server icon's and the channel's menus. **Sounds** turns sound effects on or off, sets their volume, and lets you preview and switch off each message and call sound.

### Voice & Video

- **Voice** — pick your microphone and speaker side by side and set their volumes. **Mic Test** opens the microphone without joining a call, lights the bar meter as you speak and plays your voice back until you click **Stop Testing** (in a call, the meter shows the call's own microphone instead). **Input Profile** chooses Voice Isolation (noise suppression, echo cancellation and gain control on), Studio (all off) or Custom (each on its own control). **Input Sensitivity** sets the speaking threshold, drawn yellow below and green above, with your live level over it. Push-to-talk and its key are here, and **Show Advanced Voice Settings** holds automatic gain control and high quality audio.
- **Camera** — **Test Video** previews your camera; choose the camera, resolution and frame rate.
- **Streaming** — screen share resolution and frame rate, shared audio (Chrome and Edge on a computer) and smooth-motion priority.
- **Sounds** — the voice sounds (deafen, mute, joins and leaves, streams), each with **Preview Sound**.
- **Soundboard** — how loud soundboard sounds are for you.
- **Advanced** — relay-only voice, and when to mark you away in a voice channel.

### Appearance

**Theme** picks a preset and an accent colour. **Messages** shows or hides link previews. **Chat Box** converts emoticons as you type and sets the default emoji skin tone. **Streamer Mode** hides your notes and invite codes and silences notifications. See [Themes and Appearance](themes.md) for details.

### Accessibility

Zoom level, compact or default message display, colour saturation and reduced motion.

### System

Your keybinds, the tray behaviour on Windows and Linux, updates (outside store builds), settings backup, the server directory and the app tour.

### Connected Apps

Link external accounts or services to your profile. Connected accounts may be visible to other users on your profile.

### Developer

Developer Mode and the local MCP server (desktop builds only).

## Edit Profile

**Edit Profile** (from the settings sidebar, your profile card or the account card) opens the profile editor: the panel at the left changes your avatar, avatar background and profile theme; the card at the right shows your profile as others see it, and your display name, pronouns and bio are edited right on it. Changes wait for **Save** in the "Don't forget to save your changes!" bar; **Reset** undoes them. While you have unsaved changes the editor will not close — the bar flashes red instead. Use **Main Profile ▾** to switch to the profile of another server you are connected to.
